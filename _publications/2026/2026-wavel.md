abstract_en: >-
  Wavel presents a compilation system for wafer-scale accelerators that makes executable schedules explicit, uses locality-preserving CUT and TUNE primitives, and evaluates physical cost during search. The reported results show higher throughput than expert-optimized WaferLLM and substantial speedups over a Full-Chip baseline on WSE-3 workloads.
abstract_zh: >-
  Wavel 针对晶圆级加速器中的布局、通信与执行顺序问题，引入显式可执行计划和保持局部性的 CUT/TUNE 原语，并在搜索过程中纳入物理代价评估。实验表明，Wavel 在 WSE-3 上取得了优于专家优化 WaferLLM 的吞吐表现，并相较 Full-Chip 基线实现了明显加速。
